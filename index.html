01 The Shop

A room that takes
the work seriously.

Seven chairs, hard light, and a rule nobody breaks: the cut leaves when it’s finished. We opened on Alameda in 2026 because the neighbourhood had plenty of places to get a haircut and not many to get the haircut.

Every barber in here came up through an apprenticeship — ours or someone else’s. No chair rents to anyone who can’t hold a line. That’s the whole business model.

02 Services

Priced by the work,
not by the minute.

Walk in and we’ll fit you if there’s a gap. Book and the chair is held. Either way you get the same cut.

  1. 01

    Signature Fade

    Low, mid or high. Clipper work, blended by hand, finished with a razor line-up and hot towel.

    45 min$45
  2. 02

    Skin Fade & Design

    Down to the scalp, clean at the neckline, with freehand design work if you want it.

    60 min$60
  3. 03

    Beard Sculpt & Line-Up

    Shaped to your jaw, not to a template. Straight-razor edges, oiled and finished.

    30 min$35
  4. 04

    Hot Towel Straight Razor Shave

    Three towels, pre-shave oil, single blade, cold finish. The reason people book a Saturday off.

    45 min$50
  5. 05

    The Full Send

    Cut, beard and shave in one sitting. Ninety minutes, one barber, no handoffs.

    90 min$95
  6. 06

    Kids Cut 12 & under

    Patient chair, cartoon on the mirror screen, and a first-cut certificate if it’s their first.

    30 min$30
